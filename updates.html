  1. Version 1.4

    November 5, 2026Latest

    Lighter, smoother, better together.

    About a third of the energy

    With a song playing, the window uses about a third of the processor it did, and learning a song waits for power instead of your battery.

    A smoother lock screen

    Learning never runs while the lyrics are on the lock screen, so they draw at full frame rate.

    Each listener, on your screen

    Under your party, each person's line says which Afterglow they run and whether their sound is in step.

    Learned songs take human timing

    Songs learned by listening switch to people's own word timing when it's the same recording, keeping what they learned.

    Updates during a party

    They install between songs, and you rejoin the same party by yourself after.

  2. Version 1.3.1

    November 4, 2026

    Listen along with friends, lit by the album.

    Listen along

    Everyone with Afterglow open near you shows up by first name with their song, in a menu bar item of its own. Join anyone's song and your Mac lights up with its lyrics, colours and beat, or plays it on your own Spotify in step with theirs. A Mac that sleeps or drops off Wi-Fi comes back to the same party by itself.

    Friends

    Add people nearby and see what they're playing wherever they are. Requests come to you, and a Mac is seen as soon as Local Network is allowed.

    The app, lit by the album

    Every page takes the album's colours, with a spinning record, beat bars, settings you can see as pictures, quick controls in the menu bar and a new constellation logo.

    In step on both Macs

    Playing the song on both Macs lines the two sounds up by their whole shape, allows for the third of a second Spotify stands still after a seek, and asks before a song of your own takes you out of a party.

    Word timing, first play and learned

    NetEase's word-by-word timing is found again for first plays, and checked against what Afterglow hears.

    Updates install themselves

    Checked every half hour and installed between songs, never during a party.

    Lighter on your Mac

    Settings pages use 2% of a core instead of a quarter, a 37 MB-a-song leak is fixed, and 30 frames a second on low battery.

    Also

    • Lyrics in Latin letters, for Telugu, Hindi, Korean and other scripts.
    • Pick the lock-screen look in the preview itself.
    • Save the constellation as an image.
    • ⌘1 to ⌘5 switch pages.

  3. Version 1.0

    November 3, 2026

    Two lock-screen looks, one installer, shared timing.

    Two looks for the lock screen

    The constellation alone on black, or under the player card over your wallpaper.

    One installer

    One Afterglow.pkg that replaces older copies and keeps your settings.

    Shared timing

    Word times one Mac learns time everyone else's first play.

    Every song learned in one play

    Whisper reads each song's own lyrics along the play.

    Also

    • A smoother setup.
    • Every play grades its own timing.
    • A seek slider on the lock screen.
    • This website.
