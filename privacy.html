Privacy

Afterglow works on your Mac, and the sound never leaves it. Here's everything that does, and how to stop it.

Stays on your Mac

  • The soundHeard through macOS, never the microphone. Held in memory while a song plays, then dropped. Never saved or sent.
  • What it learnedEach song's word timing and how far off each play was.
  • Lyrics and WhisperCached lyrics and the speech model.
  • Your keys and settingsSpotify keys in your Keychain, and your settings.

Leaves your Mac

  • Shared timing

    To Afterglow's database

    When each word was sung, with a fingerprint of the lyric file, the song's length and a random id for this Mac. And how far off each play was, with the speaker type and app version; these reports also count how many Macs use Afterglow each week. Never audio, lyrics, song names or an account.

    Off under Lyrics, Share timing. Forget what this Mac shared deletes it all.

  • Lyrics lookups

    To LRCLIB

    Title, artist, album and length, once per song.

    Needed for lyrics.

  • More lyrics

    To NetEase Cloud Music

    Title and main artist, when LRCLIB has nothing or NetEase has word timing.

    Off under Setup, Lyrics sources.

  • Spotify's catalogue

    To Spotify

    The playing track's id, with your developer keys.

    Only if you connect Spotify; Remove under Setup.

  • Album artwork

    To Spotify's image server

    Spotify's cover, to colour the light. Apple Music's comes from your Mac.

    Part of playing in Spotify.

  • Updates

    To GitHub

    An hourly check; new versions install between songs. Version 1.1 checks daily and asks first.

    From version 1.2.

  • Whisper

    To Hugging Face

    The speech model, downloaded once: 627 MB.

    Once, during setup.

  • Macs nearby

    To Afterglow on Macs near you

    Your first name, your Mac's name, the song playing (title and artist) and a tag your friends' Macs recognise, to anyone nearby with Afterglow open. A Mac that joins gets the song's title, artist, cover, position, beat and loudness, Mac to Mac. Never through the internet, never audio.

    From version 1.2; your name and the song from 1.3. Off in the menu bar's Listen along item, Visible to people nearby.

  • Friends

    To Afterglow's database

    Your first name and the song playing (name, artist, cover), for friends you've added. Kept under a random id only your friends get; nothing else can read it. Never audio.

    From version 1.2. Off in the menu bar's Listen along item, Friends see my song anywhere; Forget what this Mac shared deletes it.

To stop one Mac flooding the database, it keeps a scrambled, one-way form of a sender's address for a day. The app's key can't browse it: it can only send this Mac's times, fetch a song's combined times and delete what this Mac sent.

This website

Hosted on GitHub Pages, with fonts from Google Fonts. Trying a song asks LRCLIB for its lyrics and iTunes Search for its cover, from your browser, and remembers its colours there. No cookies, analytics or tracking.

Questions go to GitHub.
