What Afterglow does

  • The edge light

    Glows in the album's colours and pulses with the kick drum, even when you lock.

  • Your lock screen

    Every word lights a star as it's sung, alone on black or under the player.

  • Timed to the singer

    It listens once, on your Mac, and remembers when each word was sung.

  • Listen along

    Join a friend's Mac nearby: their song, lyrics and beat light up yours.

Ready in three minutes

  1. Install

    One line in Terminal.

  2. Set up

    Allow Spotify or Music, and your Mac's sound.

  3. Play and lock

    Play a song, then press ⌃⌘Q.
