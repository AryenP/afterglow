What you need

  • A Mac with Apple siliconM1 or later
  • macOS 14.2 or laterSonoma, Sequoia or newer
  • Spotify or Apple MusicThe Mac app, free or paid
  • About 1 GB freeFor Whisper, which times the words

Step by step

  1. Paste one line into Terminal

    Open Terminal (in Applications, Utilities), paste this and press Return:

    curl -fsSL https://aryenp.github.io/afterglow/install.sh | sh

    It checks the app is signed by Afterglow, puts it in Applications and opens it. Read the script · Use the installer instead

  2. Follow Afterglow's setup

    Four short steps:

    • Read what's playing. Allow Spotify and Music.
    • Hear the beat. Allow your Mac's sound. Never the microphone.
    • Connect Spotify. Optional: more lyrics, with Premium. How
    • Time every word. Whisper downloads once, in the background.

  3. Play a song and lock your Mac

    Lock with ⌃ ⌘ Q, or press ⌘ L in Afterglow to preview.

    After one play, every word lights as it's sung.

Connecting Spotify

Spotify's song details find lyrics other searches miss. It takes a small app of your own on Spotify's developer site, with Premium. Afterglow walks you through it:

  1. Create an app on Spotify for Developers. Any name will do.
  2. Paste the address Afterglow shows under Redirect URIs.
  3. Tick Web API and save.
  4. Copy its Client ID and Client Secret into Afterglow. They stay in your keychain.

Updating

Afterglow updates itself between songs, so it never cuts one off. To look now: Setup, Check now. On 1.0, run the Terminal line again. Settings and learned songs stay. What's new

Uninstalling

Turn off Open at login in Afterglow's Setup, then paste this into Terminal:

osascript -e 'quit app "Afterglow"'
  rm -rf /Applications/Afterglow.app
  rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/Afterglow ~/Library/Caches/xyz.afterglow.app
  defaults delete xyz.afterglow.app
  security delete-generic-password -s xyz.afterglow.spotify
  tccutil reset All xyz.afterglow.app
  pkgutil --forget xyz.afterglow.app.installer

The app, Whisper and learned timing, cached lyrics, settings, Spotify keys, permissions and the installer's receipt.

If something's off

macOS says “Afterglow.pkg” can't be opened

Afterglow isn't notarized. Use the Terminal line, or Open Anyway under Privacy & Security.

Nothing happens when a song plays

It needs permission to read Spotify and Music. Afterglow's Setup page shows what's missing and opens the right place.

The light breathes but doesn't pulse

Allow System Audio Recording under Privacy & Security. If it's on, Spotify may be playing on another device.

No lyrics on the lock screen

It uses a private macOS feature an update could close. Setup says whether it's available; the edge light works either way.

The lock screen is dark and I can't see the password field

That's the Constellation look. Touch any key and the dark lifts for a few seconds.

Lyrics are a little early or late

The second play is better. If one pair of headphones is always off, press ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ S as a word begins. Or use Lyrics, Fine-tune the timing.
